========================================================================================
SECTION 2: METHODOLOGY & APPLIED STANDARDS
========================================================================================
1. Heavy Metals Screening:
   Inductively Coupled Plasma Optical Emission Spectrometry (ICP-OES) according to IEC 62321-5:2013
   Determination of Lead (Pb), Cadmium (Cd), and Total Chromium (Cr).
2. Mercury Determination:
   Direct Mercury Analysis by Thermal Decomposition, Amalgamation, and Atomic Absorption Spectrophotometry
   according to IEC 62321-4:2013+AMD1:2017.
3. Organic Halogens & Phthalates:
   Gas Chromatography-Mass Spectrometry (GC-MS) pursuant to EN 50581 / IEC 62321-8:2017.
